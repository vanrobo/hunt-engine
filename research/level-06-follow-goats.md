# Question: Follow the Goat
**Level:** 7  
**Range:** ~2600  
**Theme:** Kanye West × Reddit × Constructed Languages × Internet Archaeology

---

## Clue

Players are presented with the encrypted phrase:

```
vflhqwrorjBH vqrr
```

---

## Solving Steps

- Decrypting the starter text reveals:

```
ScientologYE snoo
```

The unusual capitalization of **YE** points directly toward **Kanye West (Ye)**, while **Snoo** is the mascot of **Reddit**.

- Searching for **ScientologYE** leads players to Ye's unreleased song **"Tom Cruise"**, which originated from the *ScientologYE* era.

- Since **Snoo** hints at Reddit, players are expected to Google the song and eventually find the following thread:

> https://www.reddit.com/r/WestSubEver/comments/r2zltg/its_wild_that_the_universe_was_deprived_of_a/

- Under the thread is a comment left by the organiser:

> *"the saint yeezy version is good"*

This directs players toward the **Saint Yeezy** upload of the song on YouTube.

- In the comments of the Saint Yeezy upload is a hidden **Pastebin** backlink. `r07kj1GP`

- Another comment reads:

> **"Pass with Miss Hill."**

This hints that the Pastebin password is:

> **icantwait** (kanyes only collab with her)

- Inside the Pastebin are three images:

  - An image of the **Midsummer ** (21 June) (ibb link)
  - A **minus sign**
  - The **movie sevens poster** (tinyurl)

Taken together, they form:

> **21 June − 7 = 14 June**

- The Pastebin tags contain another hidden backlink leading to a **Google Doc**, along with the number **2016**.

The resulting date is:

> **14 June 2016**

- The Google Doc is titled:

> **okrand**

This references **Marc Okrand**, the creator of the **Atlantean alphabet** used throughout the document.

- Decoding the Atlantean text reveals:

> *"the number where the genius mentioned us"*

Another hidden message elsewhere in the document reads:

> *"the answer ye seek is a number"*

This establishes that:

- **Genius** refers to **Genius Lyrics**.
- **Ye** refers to Kanye West.
- The answer is expected to be a **number**, not a lyric.

- Players search **Saint Pablo** on Genius, as it was released on **14 June 2016**.

- The decoded message points them toward the lyric:

> *"Skate on the paradigm..."*

- Clicking a lyric on Genius opens its annotation, whose ID appears in the browser's address bar.

For this lyric, the URL becomes:

> `genius.com/8719281`

The hidden instruction **"the number where the genius mentioned us"** refers to this annotation ID.

---

# Final Answer

**8719281**
