lvl - 2
**Theme:** Science Fiction × ARG × Internet Mysteries × Music

## qtext

Players are given the riddle:

> *He borrowed a god's face without kneeling,*  
> *and raised thunder from a wheel no temple had blessed.*  
>
> *Elsewhere, a city meant for no audience*  
> *was built first in ink, then carried through customs*  
> *by men whose profession was pretending.*  
>
> *Find the title shared by the false heaven,*  
> *the unfinished spectacle,*  
> *and the man who made divinity look like machinery.*

---

## Solving Steps

- The riddle points toward:

> **Lord of Light**

The references include the novel itself, the abandoned film adaptation, and the famous CIA connection through **Argo**.

---

- Searching for **Lord of Light** reveals the alias of Lord of Light (Game of Thrones):

> **redgod**

This unlocks the next backlink:

> **`/redgod`**

---

- The page contains the text:

> **"hodor. o hodor. hhodor? Hodor hodor. HODOR rHodor"**

- This is encoded using the **HODOR cipher**, which decrypts to:

> **"to the stars"**

---

- Searching **To The Stars** leads to:

> **To The Stars, Inc. (TTSA)**

---

- The TTSA page lists:

> **William Neil McCasland**

a U.S. general who was briefly associated with the organisation.

- Researching him reveals that he appears on the:

> **Missing Scientists Wiki**

---

- Among the most prominent entries on the wiki is:

> **Amy Eskridge**

---

- Amy Eskridge is widely associated online with her:

> **P47 / P52 theories**

This reveals the next backlink:

> **`/p47p52`**

---

- The page contains:

- An **SSTV-encoded audio file** which decodes into an image of a rabbit.
- An obfuscated string which, when opened in a text editor, reveals:

```
/s/aGlnaGxpZ2h0OjE3ODgxNTAwOTQ1NjEyNzU3
```

---

- Decoding the Base64 component reveals an Instagram **highlight** identifier.

- Opening the highlight leads to:

> **A Travis Scott concert highlight.**

---

- From Travis Scott's Spotify profile, players are led toward:

> **Odyssey**

and then to the song:

> **When I'm Home**

---

- On the Spotify mobile app, another artist page associated with:

> **in Song DNA**

contains the profile image of the same rabbit seen in the SSTV image.

- The artist is named:

> **Christopher Nolan**

has the same rabbit PFP
and has only a single track:

> **Echoes of Your Name**
