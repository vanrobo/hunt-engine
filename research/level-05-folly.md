# Question: TFolly
**Level:** 3
**Range:** 1500
**Theme:** Modern Alchemy × Internet Personalities × Geography × Medicine

---

## Clue

Players are given a **GitHub Gist** written as an ancient piece of folklore describing the life of an alchemist named **Conoros**.

The prose never directly mentions gold, Connor Murphy, or modern medicine. Instead, it relies entirely on symbolic alchemical language.

---

## Solving Steps

- The story revolves around an alchemist named:

> **Conoros**

The name is intentionally chosen to resemble **Connor** without making the connection immediately obvious.

- Throughout the text are several descriptions of an unnamed substance:

  - *"A body which neither rusts nor bows before flame."*
  - *"The Flesh of the Sun."*
  - *"The Blood of the King."*

These all describe **gold** through its classical alchemical symbolism rather than naming it outright.

---

- Additional passages include:

> *"He received it not by the mouth, but by hidden paths known only to physicians."*

This hints that the substance was **injected intravenously**, rather than consumed.

Another line reads:

> *"Pale splendour flowed through his veins."*

which refers to **gold compounds circulating through the bloodstream**.

---

- The text also contains the classic alchemical phrase:

> *"Lead longs to become the King."*

This references the traditional goal of alchemy:

> **Transmuting lead into gold.**

---

- The final paragraph cryptically describes the alchemist's death:

  - **Crab** → Cancer → **July**
  - **Seventh dawn** → **7 July**
  - **Kingdom of the White Beast** → **Thailand**, referencing the White Elephant.

Putting these clues together identifies:

> **Connor Murphy**

who died on **7 July in Thailand**.

The final realization comes from Connor Murphy's well-known belief that **injecting gold** was a hidden practice used by elites, and that it would transform the body.

---

- This identification unlocks the backlink:

> **/connormurphy**

---

- The page contains the image:

> `https://files.catbox.moe/3em0cn.png`

- Opening the image in **Notepad** reveals the hidden string:

```
txu23ebh4jk
```

- Applying an **Atbash cipher** converts it into the geohash:

```
gcf23vys4qp
```

---

- Resolving the geohash points to:

> **County Donegal, Ireland**

- Alongside the image is the phrase:

> **"You have heard of Jack the Ripper, but not 'Jack the Ripper'."**

This is intended to confuse the solver into searching for the phrase alongside the Donegal location.

---

- The image itself is the painting:

> **Bridge of Life**

Searching for:

> **Donegal + Bridge of Life + Jack the Ripper**

eventually leads players to:

> **The Silver Sisterhood**

---

- On the Wikipedia page for the **Silver Sisterhood**, one of the notable members mentioned is the English writer:

> **Miss Martindale**

This naturally leads to the backlink:

> **/martindale**

---

- The page reveals the string:

```
DYL9nlgga7v
```

which is an **Instagram backlink**.

---

- The Instagram posts contains the hashtags:

> **#martindale**
>
> **#557**

- This points players toward **page 557** of the **Martindale: The Complete Drug Reference**.

> **Note:** Searching Martindale directly is paid, but numerous complete mirrored copies of the original edition exist online. The intended source is the **trungtamthuoc** mirror.

---

- On page **557**, players find the medicine:

> **Anakinra**

The instagram post is of  **Noxscape Star Wars poster**, so hunters are expected search related to star wars (Anakin is like the second most famous name)

- **Anakin**
- **Anakinra**

# Final Answer

**anakinra**
