# Question: Holly Timber
**Level:** 3
**Range:** ~500
**Theme:** Film × Television × Literature × David Lynch

---

## Clue

Players are [given an image drive](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1RXSWirty8tipqXPurrwGXRwT0i2pBXFs?usp=sharing) of a **Thin Blue Line** with hidden encoded text, drive folder name is `describe`:

> **"oh and no beans"**

---

## Solving Steps

- Decoding the hidden text gives:

> **"describe, oh and no beans"** -> image is of a thin blue line

- The phrase **"no beans"** is intended to eliminate the wrong interpretation.

Searching **The Thin Blue Line** returns two major results:

- **The Thin Blue Line (1988 documentary)** ✅
- **The Thin Blue Line (British sitcom)** ❌

The sitcom stars **Rowan Atkinson (Mr. Bean)**, hence the clue **"no beans"** directs players away from it and toward the documentary.

---

- The documentary **The Thin Blue Line** was produced by:

> **Miramax**

This leads to the hidden backlink:

> **`/miramax`**

---

- The `/miramax` page contains the phrase:

> **"To be in 2000 or not be in 2000."**

This references **Hamlet**, specifically the **2000 film adaptation** produced by **Miramax**.

---

- Inspecting the page source reveals the hidden text:

> **"21 wtc ki maa"**

The clue **21 WTC** refers to the **2021 ICC World Test Championship Final**.

The **Man of the Match** was:

> **Kyle Jamieson**

---

- Returning to the previous clue, players identify that **Hamlet (2000)** stars:

> **Kyle MacLachlan**

The shared first name **Kyle** bridges the cricket clue to the film.

---

- Kyle MacLachlan is best known for starring in:

> **Twin Peaks**

This unlocks the next backlink:

> **`/twinpeaks`**

---


- While the page is open, **"I'm Deranged"** plays in the background and the filename its playing from is words on the grave

The song was famously used by:

> **David Lynch**
---

- The words on grave clue is now important


Searching for **David Lynch's grave** reveals the inscription on his headstone:

> **"Night Blooming Jasmine."**

---

# Final Answer

**nightbloomingjasmine**
