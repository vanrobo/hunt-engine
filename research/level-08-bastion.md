# Question: Church
**Level:** 7  
**Range:** ~2600  
**Theme:** Football × Internet Culture × Geography × Recent Internet Controversies

---

## Clue

Players are given a video of someone performing the **Trump Dance** with a football.

The video title is:

> **"aaoge tum kabhi meri jaan keh rahi"**

---

## Solving Steps

- The title references the song **Aaoge Tum Kabhi** by **The Local Train**.

- The song became widely associated with **India's 2023 Cricket World Cup Final loss**, with countless edits appearing after the defeat on:

> **19 November 2023**

- Searching for notable events  on **19/11** reveals that **Christian Pulisic** performed the **Trump Dance** after scoring.

---

- Players now investigate **Christian Pulisic**.

- Pulisic plays for:

> **AC Milan**

- AC Milan's principal sponsor is:

> **Emirates**

This naturally leads to the hidden backlink:

> **`/emirates`**

---

- The `/emirates` page contains three seemingly unrelated clues:

  - An image of **Central Cee**
  - A snippet of **Java** code
  - The number:

```
0502232830381105022337054123325514493222
```

- The number is encrypted using a **Homophonic Cipher**.

- Decoding it gives:

> **fivesixfivefivethree**

or

> **56553**

---

- Combining all three clues gives:

> **Central + Java + 56553**

Searching this leads to:

> **Gereja Ayam (The Chicken Church)**

The location is further hinted by the page title containing the word:

> **"church"**

---

- Visiting the hidden backlink:

> **`/gerejaayam`**

reveals a page containing only:

> **"Bello!"**

displayed with **baby blue** text color.

---

- The final clues must now be interpreted together.

  - **Baby Blue** → **Bonnie Blue**
  - **Bello!** → Minion language (Minionese)

- These two references point toward the recent viral internet controversy where viewers mistakenly believed a Minion said:

> **"Bonnie Blue"**

- The actual phrase was identified as:

> **"Omid Rajabalipour"**

---

# Final Answer

**Omid Rajabalipour**
