## Solving Steps

- Inspecting the video's title reveals the word:

> **oneword.mp4**

- Watching the video carefully, one word is much more prominent than the rest:

> **helicopter**

---

- Searching **Helicopter** leads to the song:

> **"Helicopter" by A$AP Rocky**

- his real name

> **Rakim Athelaston Mayers**

This naturally leads to the backlink:

> **`/rakimathelstonmayers`**

---

- The page contains:

  - A **book cipher - pastebin/mUGZUfUH** (in a pastebin with password "ishowspeed") (in the alt string of gif)
  - A GIF of **IShowSpeed's Argentina match reaction**

The GIF itself is a decoy mostly.

---

- Extracting the hidden text message from the GIF reveals: (hex editor `strings` anything works)

> **txt @ medhanshk.com**

This is not referring to a text file, but to the domain's **TXT DNS records**.

---

- Looking up the **TXT records** for **medhanshk.com** using any DNS lookup service reveals:

```
978-0-618-05707-8
```

- The number is the ISBN for:

> **The Origin of Consciousness in the Breakdown of the Bicameral Mind**
>
> by **Julian Jaynes**

---

- Using the provided **book cipher** with this book as the key decodes the message:

> **"Our society operates on guilt."**

---

- Searching this quote leads to:

> **Frank Herbert**

specifically from:

> **Dune: Genesis**

This unlocks the next backlink:

> **`/dunegenesis`**

---

- The page contains the quote:

> *"Here lies a toppled god —*
>
> *His fall was not a small one.*
>
> *We did but build his pedestal,*
>
> *A narrow and tall one."*

---

- Although presented as a clue from **Dune: Genesis**, the quote actually points toward the **Tleilaxu sayings** found throughout the Dune universe, particularly **Dune Messiah**.
